Interests

I used to write about space for Supercluster.com. SpaceX drew me back into space after the Shuttle retuired.

I'm play tennis competitively (NTRP 4.5) and lift weights. I also love running on trails and hiking. I've been told I'm a good cook (italian, indian), if I have enough time.

Asimov's Foundation and Robot series had the greatest impact on my life, which is why I work on superintelligence. Waymos and robotaxis should have self-defense rights!
